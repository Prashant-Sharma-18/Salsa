## What we collect
When you join [program name] we collect:
- Your name and email address, and optionally your phone number.
- Your points balance and history: purchases recorded at our locations (amount and date), rewards redeemed, referrals and adjustments.
- Your marketing preference and when you set or changed it.
- If you joined through a friend's link, which member referred you.

We do **not** collect or store payment card details in the loyalty program.

## How we use it
- To run the program: record points, let you redeem rewards, and track referrals.
- To send essential program messages, such as sign-in links.
- To send offers and news **only if you opted in**. You can opt out at any time from your rewards page.
- To understand program performance in aggregate.
